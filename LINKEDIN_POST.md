I recently built a consulting-style strategy project on Vietnam's residential real estate sector under public policy uncertainty.

Instead of forecasting one recovery path, the project asks:

How should developers adapt product, land bank, capital, and launch strategies under multiple policy scenarios from 2026-2030?

The project includes:

- A sourced policy timeline from official public sources
- A rule-based Policy Uncertainty Index
- A three-scenario strategy matrix
- Developer archetype mapping for selected listed real estate companies
- A strategic memo with recommendations and monitoring indicators

My main takeaway:

Vietnam's real estate recovery should not be viewed as one uniform market rebound. It is likely to be uneven, policy-contingent, and developer-specific. Developers with legal clarity, capital discipline, credible brands, and real-demand product positioning are better placed to recover first.

This project helped me practice market structure analysis, public policy analysis, scenario planning, and consulting-style communication.
