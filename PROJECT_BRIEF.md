# Project Brief: Vietnam Residential Real Estate Strategy Under Policy Uncertainty

## 1. Project Purpose

This is a consulting-style strategy project analyzing how public policy uncertainty affects residential real estate developers in Vietnam during 2026-2030. It focuses on product strategy, land bank strategy, capital strategy, and launch strategy under multiple policy paths.

## 2. Business Question

How should Vietnamese residential real estate developers adapt their product, land bank, capital, and launch strategies under public policy uncertainty during 2026-2030?

## 3. Why It Matters

Vietnam's real estate sector is strongly shaped by land law, housing regulation, credit policy, corporate bond regulation, social housing programs, infrastructure execution, and market transparency rules. These policy levers influence project execution, buyer demand, funding access, and market structure.

## 4. Method

1. Build a sourced policy timeline.
2. Code policy events by area, expected direction, and uncertainty level.
3. Convert uncertainty levels into a rule-based Policy Uncertainty Index.
4. Build three policy scenarios.
5. Classify selected listed developers into strategy archetypes.
6. Translate the analysis into strategic recommendations.

## 5. Core Insight

Vietnam's real estate recovery should not be treated as one uniform market rebound. It is likely to be uneven, policy-contingent, and developer-specific. Developers with legal clarity, capital discipline, credible brands, and real-demand product positioning are better placed to recover first.

## 8. Limitations

- The Policy Uncertainty Index is rule-based and not official.
- This is not an investment recommendation.
- The project uses public sources only.
- Scenario planning is not forecasting.
- Company classifications should be updated as new disclosures and policy implementation evidence become available.
